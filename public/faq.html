Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Booking, delivery, setup, and more.

Booking

How do I book?

Choose your event date, pick your dropoff and pickup windows, add your items to the cart, and pay at checkout. You can also call or text 702-706-2455.

How far in advance should I book?

Online booking is open as early as the next day. For weekends and holidays, we recommend booking at least two weeks ahead, because popular items can run out.

How do I pay?

You pay in full by card at checkout, through Stripe's secure payment page.

Can I cancel or change my date?

Yes. Your refund depends on how much notice you give. See our Rental Policy for details.

Delivery and pickup

How much is delivery?

Delivery is a flat $25, which covers both dropoff and pickup. Our standard delivery includes dropoff and pickup only. Please reach out to us if you'd like to add setup/teardown to your order. Our setup/teardown fee will typically be an additional 30% of your order total.

Do you set everything up?

Our standard delivery includes dropoff and pickup only. Please reach out to us if you'd like to add setup/teardown to your order. Our setup/teardown fee will typically be an additional 30% of your order total.

What areas do you serve?

We deliver across Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, Spring Valley, and Enterprise. Checkout will confirm your ZIP code. If you aren't sure, ask us.

When will you drop off and pick up?

When you book, you'll choose from the windows available for your date, usually a morning window (7 to 11 AM) or an evening window (5 to 8 PM). Dropoff can be up to two days before your event and pickup up to two days after, depending on the day. If you need something different, add a note at checkout and we'll try to make it work.

Do you charge by the day?

No. The listed price covers your event and the dropoff and pickup windows you choose when you book.

Can I pick up the items myself?

All orders are currently delivered by our team, so we don't offer customer pickup.

Do I need to be home for dropoff and pickup?

Not always. If nobody will be there, add a note at checkout telling us where to leave the items and we'll let you know if that works. Please make sure items are accessible for pickup.

During and after your event

Do I need to clean the rentals?

You don't need to wash anything. Just remove trash and food, and have everything gathered for pickup. Extra cleaning fees only apply to heavy soiling or permanent stains.

What happens if something gets damaged?

Tell us as soon as you can. You're responsible for reasonable repair or replacement costs, as explained in our Rental Policy.

What if the weather turns bad?

Contact us before your dropoff and we'll reschedule at no charge, based on availability.

Still have a question?

Contact us or call or text 702-706-2455.
