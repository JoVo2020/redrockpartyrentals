Policies

Rental Policy

Cancellations, refunds, damage, and late returns.

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This policy applies to every Red Rock Party Rentals LLC booking and is part of our Terms of Service. To cancel or change a booking, call or text 702-706-2455 or email booking@redrockpartyrentals.com. We count your notice from when we receive it.

Cancellations and refunds

When you cancelWhat you receive
7 or more days before your eventFull refund
2 to 6 days before your event50% refund, or a full credit toward a future date
Less than 48 hours before your eventNo refund. We may offer a credit at our discretion.

Refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to post them.

If we have to cancel your booking, you'll receive a full refund.

Changing your date

You can move your booking to a new date once at no charge, as long as you give us at least 48 hours' notice and your items are available on the new date.

Bad weather

If bad weather threatens your event, contact us before your dropoff and we'll reschedule at no charge, based on availability. Once items have been dropped off, the cancellation terms above apply.

Dropoff and access

Please make sure we can reach the delivery spot during your dropoff window. If we can't deliver because the location can't be reached or nobody can be contacted, we treat the booking as a cancellation made less than 48 hours before the event.

Taking care of your rentals

You're responsible for rented items from dropoff until we pick them up, including if they're lost, stolen, or damaged.

  • Damage or loss: we'll charge the reasonable cost to repair or replace the item. We'll send photos and an invoice after pickup, because we don't keep your card on file.
  • Cleaning: normal use is fine, and you don't need to wash anything. We may charge a cleaning fee for heavy soiling, or a replacement fee for permanently stained linens.
  • Weather: bring rented items under cover if rain or high winds are expected, and don't leave them where they could blow over or get damaged.

Pickup and late returns

Please have all items gathered and accessible by the start of your pickup window. If items aren't ready when we arrive, we may charge a $25 return-trip fee, plus the rental price of those items for each extra day we can't collect them.

Safe use

Use every item only as intended and follow any instructions we provide. Please place tables, tall games, and equipment on stable, level ground, and keep children supervised around them. Ask us if you aren't sure how to use something safely.

Questions

We're happy to work with you when something unexpected comes up. Contact us and we'll do our best to help.
