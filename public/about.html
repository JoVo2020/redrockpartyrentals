About Us

Family-Owned, Las Vegas Local

Your party, without the hassle

Red Rock Party Rentals is a family-owned business serving the Las Vegas area. We make it easy to get what you need for backyard birthdays, baby showers, graduations, and family celebrations.

You choose your date and items online, and we bring everything to your door and pick it up when you're done. That leaves you free to enjoy your event with your guests.

What you can count on

  • Simple booking: see what's available for your date and book online in minutes.
  • Clear pricing: per-item prices and a flat $25 fee for delivery and pickup.
  • Local service: we've served more than 200 events across the Las Vegas area.
  • Real communication: a local family business you can call or text.
