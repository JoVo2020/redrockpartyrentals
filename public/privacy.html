Privacy Notice

Effective date: January 31, 2026

This notice explains how Red Rock Party Rentals LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information through our website, redrockpartyrentals.com, and our rental services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and information collected automatically when you use our website.

  • Contact details: Your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Delivery details: Your delivery address, event date, drop-off and pickup times, and any additional notes you provide.
  • Order details: The rental items you reserve, quantities, pricing, payment status, and transaction history.
  • Messages: Information you provide through our contact form, email, text messages, or chat assistant.
  • Website activity: Pages you visit, interactions with our website, IP address, device and browser information, cookies, advertising identifiers, and information about how you found our website, such as advertisements or referring websites.

Payment card information is entered directly through our secure third-party payment processor, Stripe. We do not see or store your full credit or debit card number.

Information Saved in Your Browser

Our website uses your browser's local storage to save information such as your shopping cart, event date, contact details, delivery address, and how you first found our website. This helps preserve your booking information as you navigate between pages.

You can remove this information at any time by clearing your browser's website data.

Our website also uses cookies and similar technologies for analytics and advertising, as described below.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process payments, confirm reservations, and deliver and pick up rental equipment.
  • Communicate with you about your booking, including confirmations, updates, and customer support.
  • Respond to questions and requests.
  • Maintain business, accounting, and tax records.
  • Understand how visitors use our website and improve its functionality.
  • Measure the effectiveness of our advertising and marketing efforts.
  • Send promotional communications where permitted by law.
  • Prevent fraud, protect our business and customers, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Analytics, Advertising, and Website Tracking

We use third-party analytics and advertising services, including Google Analytics, Google Ads, and Meta (Facebook and Instagram), to understand website activity, measure advertising performance, and improve our marketing.

These services may collect information about your interactions with our website through cookies, tracking pixels, and similar technologies.

We may also share certain customer information, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, and booking activity, with advertising providers to help determine whether advertisements result in reservations. Contact information may be hashed before transmission or matching, depending on the service.

Third-party advertising and analytics providers may collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites or applications. Depending on the provider, your settings, and applicable law, this information may be used for analytics, advertising measurement, and personalized advertising.

You can manage certain advertising preferences through your browser settings and the privacy controls provided by Google and Meta.

Who We Share Information With

We share personal information with trusted third-party service providers that help us operate our business, including:

  • Payment processors: To securely process payments, refunds, and transactions.
  • Analytics and advertising providers: To measure website activity, evaluate advertising performance, and support our marketing efforts.
  • Order management and customer communication providers: To manage reservations, maintain customer records, answer questions, and communicate with customers.
  • Accounting and business software providers: To maintain financial records, process invoices, and support business operations.
  • Website hosting and technology providers: To operate our website, process online forms, and maintain our systems.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or in connection with a sale, merger, or transfer of our business.

We do not, and will not, sell your personal information. We do share certain information with advertising providers as described above.

Your Privacy Choices

Review, correction, or deletion

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we maintain about you.

We will review your request and respond as required by applicable law. Certain information may need to be retained for legal, accounting, tax, or legitimate business purposes.

Nevada privacy rights

We do not, and will not, sell personal information as defined under Nevada law.

Nevada residents may submit a verified request directing us not to sell their personal information by emailing booking@redrockpartyrentals.com.

Advertising and analytics

You can limit certain tracking technologies through your browser settings, including by blocking or deleting cookies.

You can also manage advertising preferences through:

  • Google Ads Settings: https://myadcenter.google.com
  • Google Analytics Opt-Out: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Facebook Ad Preferences: https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences

These controls may not prevent all data collection or advertising.

Marketing communications

If you receive promotional emails or text messages from us, you may opt out by following the unsubscribe instructions provided or contacting us directly.

Even if you opt out of promotional communications, we may still contact you regarding reservations, payments, deliveries, pickups, and other necessary service matters.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill reservations, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal, tax, and accounting requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and its purpose.

How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Payments are processed through a secure third-party payment provider rather than directly through our website.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no website, electronic transmission, or data storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Our website and rental services are intended for adults planning events.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice periodically to reflect changes in our business practices, technology, or applicable laws.

When we make changes, we will post the revised notice on this page and update the effective date above. Material changes will be communicated through an updated notice on our website or other appropriate means.

We encourage you to review this notice periodically.
