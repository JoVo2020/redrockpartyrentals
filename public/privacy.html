Privacy Notice

Effective date: January 31, 2026

This notice explains how Red Rock Party Rentals LLC ("we," "us") collects, uses, and shares personal information through redrockpartyrentals.com.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Delivery details: your street address, event date, dropoff and pickup times, and any notes you add.
  • Order details: the items you book and what you paid.
  • Messages: what you send through our contact form, by email, by text, or through our chat assistant.
  • Website activity: pages you visit, device and browser information, and how you found our site, such as an ad or a referring website.

Your card details are entered on our payment processor's secure checkout page. We don't see or store your full card number.

Information saved in your browser

Our site saves your cart, event date, contact details, delivery address, and how you first found us in your browser's local storage, so your booking isn't lost as you move between pages. You can remove this at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Google's tools may also use cookies, as described below.

How we use your information

  • To process payments, confirm bookings, and deliver and pick up your rentals
  • To contact you about your booking and answer your questions
  • To keep business, accounting, and tax records
  • To understand how people use our site and whether our advertising works
  • To prevent fraud and protect our business and customers

Who we share it with

We share information with service providers who help us run the business, including:

  • Payment processors: to take payments securely
  • Google and other analytics and advertising providers: to measure website use and advertising, including using your order contact details to see whether our ads led to a booking
  • Order management and customer communication tools: to process orders, answer messages, and run promotions
  • Accounting providers: for bookkeeping and invoicing
  • Website hosting providers: to run our website and deliver contact form messages

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or someone's safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business.

We don't sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • Review or correct your information: email us and we'll help you review, update, or delete it, unless we need to keep it for legal or accounting reasons.
  • Opt out of the sale of your information: although we do not, and will not, sell your personal information, Nevada residents may still submit an opt-out request to booking@redrockpartyrentals.com.
  • Advertising and analytics: you can manage ad personalization in your Google ad settings and opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

How long we keep it, and how we protect it

We keep information for as long as we need it to provide your rental and meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, but no website or online service is completely secure.

Children

Our site is for adults planning events. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date above.
